# Changelog

The newest `## ` section is used as the release notes when publishing (see PUBLISHING.md).

## 1.4.0

- Added support for Minecraft 1.21.10 (Fabric and NeoForge)
- Fixed pets losing their tracking after a world reload on 1.21.10
- Fixed a crash when saving the world on 1.21.10 after setting a home or binding an ocarina
- Fixed the pet preview in the Roster jittering on 1.21.10
- Fixed guardian pets not being protected from attacks by tamed mobs
- Fixed the Roster keybind not working on NeoForge
- Fixed renamed pets not updating their name in the Roster
